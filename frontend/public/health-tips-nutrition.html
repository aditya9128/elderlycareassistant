Stay Hydrated

Seniors often don't feel thirsty even when dehydrated. Keep water nearby and sip throughout the day.

  • 8 glasses daily minimum
  • Herbal teas count
  • Eat water-rich fruits

Protein Power

Adequate protein helps maintain muscle mass and strength as we age. Include protein in every meal.

  • Fish & lean meats
  • Eggs & dairy
  • Beans & lentils

Calcium & Vitamin D

Strong bones need calcium and vitamin D. Essential for preventing osteoporosis and fractures.

  • Dairy products
  • Fortified cereals
  • 15 min sunlight daily

Fiber-Rich Foods

Fiber aids digestion, helps control blood sugar, and keeps you feeling full longer.

  • Whole grains
  • Fresh vegetables
  • Oats & bran

Limit Sugar & Salt

Reduce processed foods high in sugar and sodium to protect your heart and manage blood pressure.

  • Read food labels
  • Cook at home
  • Use herbs for flavor

Small, Frequent Meals

Eating smaller portions more often helps with digestion and maintains steady energy levels.

  • 5-6 small meals daily
  • Healthy snacks
  • Don't skip meals
