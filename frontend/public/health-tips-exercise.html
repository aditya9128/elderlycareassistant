Daily Walking

Walking is the perfect low-impact exercise. Start with 10-15 minutes and gradually increase.

  • Wear supportive shoes
  • Use walking stick if needed
  • Walk with a buddy

Chair Exercises

Great for those with limited mobility. Strengthen muscles while staying safely seated.

  • Seated leg raises
  • Arm circles
  • Seated marching

Balance Training

Prevent falls with daily balance exercises. Hold onto a sturdy surface when starting.

  • Single-leg stands
  • Heel-to-toe walking
  • Side leg raises

Stretching & Flexibility

Keep your joints flexible and reduce stiffness with gentle daily stretching routines.

  • Neck rolls
  • Shoulder stretches
  • Ankle rotations

Water Exercises

Swimming and water aerobics are gentle on joints while providing excellent resistance training.

  • Water walking
  • Aqua aerobics classes
  • Pool noodle exercises

Gentle Yoga & Tai Chi

Mind-body exercises that improve balance, flexibility, and mental well-being.

  • Chair yoga
  • Slow Tai Chi movements
  • Breathing exercises
