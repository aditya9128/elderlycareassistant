Stay Socially Connected

Social isolation can lead to depression. Stay connected with family, friends, and community.

  • Daily phone calls
  • Join community groups
  • Video chat with family

Practice Meditation

Just 10 minutes of daily meditation can reduce stress, anxiety, and improve overall well-being.

  • Deep breathing
  • Guided meditation apps
  • Mindful moments

Keep Your Mind Active

Mental exercises help maintain cognitive function and may reduce dementia risk.

  • Crossword puzzles
  • Learn new skills
  • Read daily

Practice Gratitude

Focusing on positive aspects of life can significantly improve mood and outlook.

  • Keep a gratitude journal
  • Share appreciation daily
  • Reflect on positives

Prioritize Sleep

Quality sleep is essential for mental clarity, mood regulation, and overall brain health.

  • Consistent bedtime
  • Relaxing bedtime routine
  • Limit screens before bed

Engage in Hobbies

Creative activities bring joy and purpose. Pursue activities that make you happy.

  • Gardening
  • Music & arts
  • Cooking & baking
