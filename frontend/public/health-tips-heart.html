Keep your heart strong and healthy with these essential tips for seniors.

Stay Active Daily

Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate exercise daily. Walking, swimming, or gentle yoga are excellent choices for seniors.

  • Morning walks
  • Light stretching
  • Chair exercises

Heart-Healthy Diet

Fill your plate with fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins. Limit sodium and saturated fats.

  • Omega-3 rich fish
  • Leafy greens
  • Nuts and seeds

Monitor Blood Pressure

Check your blood pressure regularly. High blood pressure is a silent killer that can lead to heart disease.

  • Check twice daily
  • Keep a log
  • Share with doctor

Avoid Smoking

Smoking damages blood vessels and increases heart disease risk. It's never too late to quit.

  • Seek support groups
  • Try nicotine patches
  • Stay motivated

Quality Sleep

Aim for 7-8 hours of quality sleep. Poor sleep is linked to high blood pressure and heart disease.

  • Regular sleep schedule
  • Dark, cool room
  • Limit screen time

Manage Stress

Chronic stress increases heart disease risk. Practice relaxation techniques daily.

  • Deep breathing
  • Meditation
  • Social connections

Warning Signs - Seek Help Immediately

Chest Pain or Pressure

Shortness of Breath

Dizziness or Fainting

Irregular Heartbeat

Emergency: Call 112
